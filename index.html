Control triangulation

TraceGraph is designed to provide visibility into your actual decision surface — not just what’s documented. By triangulating decision records with runtime behavior, we identify deviations as close to when they occur as possible.

  • Decision: what was approved, by whom, and why
  • Reality: what actually changed or ran in production (code, config, runtime, vendor enablement)
  • Deviation: where behavior doesn’t align to an approved decision (ownership, evidence, or controls missing)

This turns governance from periodic documentation into operational risk telemetry.
